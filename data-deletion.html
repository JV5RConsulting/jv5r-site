Data deletion

How to remove Content Curator's access and delete your data

1. Ask us to delete your data

Email jv5rconsulting@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the name of your Facebook Page or Instagram account. We delete the account identifiers, access tokens, and publishing records we hold for it within 30 days and email you when it is done.

2. Remove our access yourself

You can revoke access at any time, without contacting us:

  • If you shared your Page or Instagram account with our business: in Meta Business Settings, go to Users → Partners, select JV5R Consulting, and remove it (or remove the shared assets).
  • If you connected through Facebook Login: on Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, find Content Curator, and remove it.

Once access is removed, Content Curator can no longer publish to your accounts.

What stays on your accounts

Posts and comments that were already published are part of your own Facebook Page and Instagram account. Deleting your data with us does not remove them; you can delete them in Meta Business Suite or the Facebook and Instagram apps.
